# Austin Cybercab Simulator

A first-person simulator set in **Austin, Texas**, where you follow a Tesla Cybercab operating as a robotaxi.

## Goal

Put you on the streets of Austin trailing an autonomous Cybercab as it picks up riders, navigates real-world style routes, and behaves like a production robotaxi. The focus is the chase / follow experience: keep the vehicle in view, learn how it drives, and explore Austin from the curb and the road.

## Planned experience

- **Setting:** Austin, TX streets and landmarks
- **Subject:** Cybercab (robotaxi mode)
- **Perspective:** You following the vehicle (on foot, in another car, or free camera)
- **Behaviors:** Idle, route following, pickup / dropoff, traffic interaction
